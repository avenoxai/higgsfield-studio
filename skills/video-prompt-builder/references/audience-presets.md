# Audience Presets — Tribe-Specific Encoding

Universal psychology mechanisms become surgical when translated into tribe-specific triggers. This file contains ready-to-use presets for the primary target audiences.

---

## Preset 1 — Crypto Twitter (CT) / Web3

### The Identity Stack (user often occupies 3-5 simultaneously)

- **The Builder-pilled** — Solo devs, protocol engineers, Foundry-heads. Self-image: "I am the only serious person left in this casino." Aesthetic: terminal green-on-black, Vim, GitHub contributions, `0x...` addresses. Respond to: craftsmanship, LOC counts, 1-guy-shipping narratives.
- **The Maxi / Tribalist** — BTC maxi, ETH aligned, Solana retard, Hyperliquid cultist. Self-image: "I picked the winning team before you." Respond to: enemy-coded content, L1 war memes, receipts.
- **The Degen / Trencher** — Pump.fun natives, memecoin snipers. Self-image: gambler-warrior, "I am cooked but I'm cooking." Respond to: chart porn, PnL screenshots, self-deprecation, nihilist humor.
- **The Aesthete (Milady / mfers)** — Post-ironic PFP holders. Self-image: "I'm the taste layer of crypto." Respond to: in-jokes, chibi/glitch aesthetics, network spirituality, anti-corporate sheen.
- **The Founder / KOL** — Shipping a product, LARPing builder+degen. Self-image: "Stoic architect." Respond to: 1-guy-in-a-room shots, quiet-money flexes, solo-empire narratives.
- **The Anon / Resistance** — Privacy-maxi, crypto-as-liberation. Respond to: "they" framing, censorship-resistance aesthetics, surveillance paranoia.

**What they claim vs. what they click:** They all think they're the serious builder. They all click the degen bait. Plan for both layers — surface narrative for the ego, hook imagery for the lizard.

### Psychological Triggers

| Trigger | Concrete expression |
|---|---|
| Greed / 100x fantasy | Candle going vertical, wallet balance incrementing, PnL time-lapse. Never stated — always shown. |
| FOMO / missed opportunity | Timestamp overlay "you had 6 hours"; chart with late entry marked; "wen u aped" |
| Tribalism | Side-by-side: our chain vs theirs. ETH gas fee screenshot vs ours. "Them" drab, "us" high-contrast. |
| Insider signaling | Ticker in corner no context, wallet address easter egg, "if you know you know" gating |
| Degen humor (sub-tribal) | Pepe = OG ETH degen; Wojak = cope/doomer/loss; Chad/Gigachad = winner/unironic flex; Milady = taste tribe; mfers = chill-doomer. **Mixing wrong signals tourist.** |
| Anti-institutional "they" | SEC logo glitching. News anchor cut off. Corporate bank logos dissolving. |
| Paranoia / resistance | CCTV aesthetic, night-vision green, scan-lines, "they can't stop this" |
| Builder-pilled grind | 3:47 AM timestamp. `pnpm build` running. Commit history. Empty Monster can. |
| 1-guy-in-a-room | Wide of empty office, single lit desk. **Highest-converting archetype of 2026 cycle.** |
| Quiet-power flex | No Lambo. Watch glimpse. Airport lounge. "Made it without telling anyone." |

### Language Rules (2026)

**Active vocab:** based, cooked, unhinged, sent it, wen, ngmi, gmi, wagmi (tired), cope, rekt, degen, trenches, ape, ser, anon, fren (declining), gm (ritualistic), alpha, mid, slop, mog, lock in / locked in, "it's over / we're so back," "number go up," "ticker coming," "CA?", "no VC," "fair launch," "stealth."

**Grammar:**
- **Lowercase default.** Caps = corporate/boomer.
- **No em-dashes.** AI slop tell. Use line breaks or slashes.
- **Emoji budget: 0-1.** 🟢🔴📈 acceptable. ✨🚀🎉 flags tourist.
- **Captions underclaim.** "small thing we shipped" on a massive drop = kayfabe. Overclaiming ("HUGE ANNOUNCEMENT 🚀🚀") = instant scroll.
- **Ticker-without-product-explanation** = flex (assumes you know).

**Authentic tonality:** dry, clipped, slightly nihilist, self-deprecating about a win.
**Cope tonality (avoid):** hype-coded, exclamation-heavy, "to the moon" energy.
**NGMI-cringe tells:** "revolutionary," "game-changing," stock footage handshakes, Canva gradients, stakeholder diagrams, any sentence with both "ecosystem" and "synergy."

### Instant-Scroll Anti-Patterns for CT

- Web2 SaaS sheen — gradients, 3D isometric illustrations, Figma mockups floating
- Stock footage handshakes, diverse team laughing, city skyline timelapse
- Announcement cliches: "We're excited to announce..."
- Over-explaining product in first 3s
- AI-slop tells: 7-fingered hands, warped text, melting watch faces, too-smooth skin, generated CEO avatars, "cinematic" lighting that's too even
- Begging: "like & RT," "follow for more alpha," "smash that bell"
- Corporate polish on a degen product
- Fake token charts (CT cross-references real-time)
- American-corporate-commercial voiceover cadence

### Aesthetic Code

**CT Degen:** Dark terminal, CRT glow, glitch, lo-fi. Hard single-source lighting, candlelit, amber/teal. Fast cuts 0.3-0.8s. Dry lowercase, no VO or ironic TTS.

**CT Builder:** Minimal mono, terminal green. Natural desk lamp, cold tones. Slower 1-2s holds. Founder's imperfect voice.

**CT Aesthete (Milady-adjacent):** Pastel, chibi/glitch mix, post-irony, network-spirit aesthetic. Medium pacing. No VO, music-driven.

### Launch Video Structure (10-20s / 6 clips on this preset)

- **C1 (0-1s hook):** One visual that IS the thesis. Token ticker as literal object (etched in metal, flashing on CCTV, tattooed). No logo card, no intro.
- **C2 (1-3s enemy/tension):** Paint what this replaces. Gas fee screenshot. Corporate logo dissolving. Viewer knows the target.
- **C3 (3-7s product reveal):** What it does, shown not told. Terminal command running. Swap executing fast. Dashboard lighting up. No explainer text.
- **C4 (7-12s tribe seeding):** An in-joke, a wink. Easter-egg wallet address. Pepe/Milady in corner. Chart only insiders catch. Converts watcher → member.
- **C5 (12-15s CTA soft):** Ticker + chain + CA in corner. No "buy now." Maybe "live." Maybe nothing.

**Caption pairing:** 1 line, lowercase, understated, slightly cryptic. "it's live" >> "Introducing $XYZ, the future of..."

**Where to drop CA:** Caption last line, no preamble. Or pinned reply. Or in-video easter egg (more based). Never opening frame (reads shill).

## Preset 2 — Masculine Self-Improvement / Philosophy / Stoicism

### The Identity Stack

- **The Stoic Grinder** — 5am wake, cold plunge, Meditations on nightstand. Self-image: "I am shaping myself against modernity." Respond to: austere aesthetic, Roman/Greek imagery, Eastern-European monk discipline, barbell + books.
- **The Monk-mode Operator** — No-fap, dopamine fast, habit tracker. Self-image: "I'm deleting the weak version of me." Respond to: 90-day transformation arcs, streak counters, silent war narratives.
- **The High-T Maxi** — Testosterone-aware, seed-oil-hostile, raw meat-adjacent. Self-image: "I'm biologically optimized." Respond to: hard-edge nutrition, sun exposure, strength work.
- **The Philosopher-Warrior** — Reads Marcus Aurelius, quotes Seneca, pairs weight training with Jordan Peterson/Huberman. Self-image: "I am the lineage of serious men." Respond to: historical imagery, statues, classical architecture, quote cards.
- **The Sigma / Anti-Social-Status** — Rejects corporate, rejects herd, anti-screen-time. Self-image: "I do not participate." Respond to: solitude imagery, forest scenes, no-phone flexes.

### Psychological Triggers

| Trigger | Expression |
|---|---|
| Enemy framing | Modernity, screens, soy, porn, seed oils, corporate cuck cubicles, "NPC" consumers |
| FOMO | "While you were doomscrolling, he was on day 90" |
| Status anxiety | "Most men will die without knowing this feeling." "You're a mid man if you..." |
| Ego-complicity | "Only 1% of men have the discipline to do this. You're one of them." |
| Tribalism | "Real men vs. the algorithm-fed masses." "Builder brothers." |
| Insider signaling | Specific books in shot (Meditations, 48 Laws, Bible, Book of Five Rings). Barbell chalk on hands. Coffee black. |
| Lineage appeal | B&W marble statues, classical columns, Roman coins, monk imagery |
| Transformation arc | 90-day journey, before/after, "day 1 → day 90" cuts |
| Dopamine-minimalism flex | No phone on table. Flip phone. Paper notebook. Mechanical watch. |

### Language Rules

**Active vocab:** discipline, based, locked in, monk mode, warrior, stoic, the work, the grind, deep work, the logos, the mission, lineage, brotherhood, sovereignty, the arena, wisdom, virtue, "memento mori," "amor fati," "the obstacle is the way."

**Grammar:**
- Short declarative sentences. Command-form voice. Fragments.
- **No emojis.** Emoji = soy signaling in this tribe.
- Quote attribution: Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epictetus, Nietzsche, Jung, Musashi. Sometimes apocryphal — the attribution matters more than accuracy.
- Voiceover (if any): deep male, slow, gravelly, "History Channel documentary" cadence. Or British philosopher cadence.

**Authentic tonality:** serious, austere, heavy, reverent.
**Cringe tells:** hype-bro energy, "LETS GOOOO," "sigma grindset" when self-applied (reads ironic), frat-bro aesthetic, fake-deep self-help pastel infographics with Comic-Sans-adjacent fonts.

### Instant-Scroll Anti-Patterns

- Pastel colors, gradients, soft fonts
- Hype-bro voiceover ("WHATS UP KINGS")
- Influencer-selfie content (reads thirsty/unserious)
- Self-applied labels ("as a sigma," "as an alpha male") — reads ironic/cringe
- "Mindset" overload — this tribe respects action, not affirmations
- Smiling talking heads (stoic tribe reveres the unsmiling face)
- Trendy gen-Z slang ("no cap," "bet") — marks you as outside the tribe
- Motivational music that sounds like Facebook reels

### Aesthetic Code

**Stoic/Philosopher:** B&W or desaturated color, marble and stone textures, high contrast chiaroscuro, blue-and-orange gym lighting or warm candle light. Medium-slow pacing, 1-2s holds. Deep male VO or no VO. Classical music, sparse piano, ambient strings, Hans Zimmer-adjacent.

**Monk-mode Operator:** Cool blue tones, clean desk, minimal environment. Natural window light. Medium pacing. Habit tracker visuals. Silent or ambient electronic.

**High-T Maxi:** Outdoor, golden sun, masculine activities (lifting, hunting, building). Warm natural lighting. Action-paced. No VO or raw grunts. Natural sound.

### Philosophy-Content Video Spine (5 clips / 40s)

- **C1 (0-8s Hook):** A single image with weight. Marble statue half-lit. Bronze sword on stone. Candle in darkness. Text overlay: one provocation — "Most men will die asleep." No intro.
- **C2 (8-16s Context):** The state of modernity as enemy. Screens glowing at 3am, fluorescent office, soft bodies, slack-jawed consumption. This is the "them" frame.
- **C3 (16-24s Alternative):** The way shown in action. Cold plunge at sunrise, heavy barbell, Meditations open, black coffee, 5am clock. No explanation.
- **C4 (24-32s The Quote):** The wisdom payload. Marcus/Seneca/Epictetus quote. Male VO reads it over slow push-in on carved stone or aged book.
- **C5 (32-40s Call to Self):** Close-up on a man's eyes. Text: "choose." Or: "begin." Or: "today."

**Caption (optional):** One quote fragment. "amor fati." "the obstacle is the way." "memento mori."

### Psychological Triggers

**Caption:** `the version of you you've been waiting for ✨`

## The Audience-Intake Questions (for custom audiences)

When the audience doesn't match a preset, answer these in writing before writing a single clip:

1. **Identity stack:** What are the 3-5 sub-tribes? Which does this video speak to?
2. **Self-image vs actual-response:** What do they claim to want vs what they click?
3. **Tribal enemies:** Who/what do they define themselves against?
4. **Insider vocabulary:** 10 words only they use; 1 word that marks an outsider on sight?
5. **Aesthetic codes:** Colors, lighting, typography that says "for me"?
6. **Cringe triggers:** What do they screenshot-and-mock?
7. **Status currency:** What does "winning" look like in this tribe?
8. **Medium-native format:** Vertical lo-fi? Cinematic 16:9? Screen recording? (Downstream of tribe, not platform.)

Only after answering these should a single prompt be written.
