# Proven Prompts — Locked-In Patterns Per Use Case

Working examples. Each pattern has been tested or derived from documented operator workflows. Adapt variables, keep structure.

## Pattern 1: Crypto-Launch Hero Clip (C5 reveal, 8s)

Use case: token/protocol launch, hero clip showing ticker lockup.

```
One continuous cinematic shot, 8 seconds, 24fps, 9:16 vertical.

SCENE: Extreme macro close-up of a polished dark metal surface. The ticker
$[TICKER] is being slowly etched into the metal by an unseen tool, each
character cutting in with a faint shower of orange sparks and a thin wisp
of smoke. The metal glows subtly from within — electric cyan light bleeding
through the letter strokes as they complete.

CAMERA: Locked-off macro, 100mm lens equivalent, shallow depth of field.
No camera motion. The frame holds still as the action plays out across
the 8 seconds.

LIGHTING: Single cold rim-light from frame-left, warm ember glow from the
etching point itself. Chiaroscuro contrast. Volumetric smoke drifts
through the frame.

STYLE: Cinematic, 35mm film grain, muted desaturated palette, industrial
aesthetic. No text overlays. No logos.

CONSTRAINTS: sharp clarity, natural colors, stable picture, no blur,
no ghosting, no flickering. The ticker characters must be crisp and
legible throughout. No additional text appears. No hands visible.
```

**Why it works:**
- Macro subject is 100% controllable — zero AI tells
- Etching motion is a "physics" action Seedance handles cleanly
- Rim-light + volumetric smoke = cinematic shield tokens
- Ticker is the signature moment — no competing visuals
- 9:16 for TikTok/X native

## Pattern 2: Solo-Founder-Room (CT builder, C2 or C3, 8s)

Use case: establishing the "1 guy in a room" archetype.

```
One continuous cinematic shot, 8 seconds, 24fps, 9:16 vertical.

SCENE: Wide low-angle shot of a darkened home office at 3 AM. A single
desk in the center of frame, illuminated only by the glow of three
monitors. Black mechanical keyboard, empty Monster can beside it. A
silhouetted figure sits at the desk, back to camera, hoodie up, typing
slowly. The clock on the wall reads 3:47.

CAMERA: Static wide establishing shot, 35mm lens. Very slight forward
dolly over the 8 seconds — 1 meter of movement total. Nothing else.

LIGHTING: Monitor glow as key light — blue-cyan on the figure's back
and the desk. No other lights. Heavy shadows swallow the rest of the
room. A thin rim-light from a window offscreen right.

STYLE: Cinematic, 35mm film grain, muted desaturated palette, blue
dominant. Amber ember from a small desk lamp adds warm contrast.

CONSTRAINTS: sharp clarity, natural colors, stable picture, no blur,
no ghosting, no flickering. The figure remains in silhouette — no face
visible. No text on monitors. Character face stable without deformation.
```

**Why it works:**
- Silhouette = no face geometry drift risk
- Single-source lighting simplifies model's job
- CT audience reads this as "based builder"
- Minimal motion = high consistency

## Pattern 3: Stoicism / Philosophy Hook (C1, 8s)

Use case: opening clip for masculine self-improvement / stoicism content.

```
One continuous cinematic shot, 8 seconds, 24fps, 9:16 vertical.

SCENE: Extreme close-up of a weathered Roman marble bust — the face of
a stern, bearded man carved in stone, eyes hollow and unmoving. The
camera slowly pushes in over 8 seconds, revealing cracks in the marble,
dust motes floating in a single shaft of amber light crossing the face.

CAMERA: Slow linear dolly push-in, starting 2 meters away, ending 0.5
meters away. 50mm lens, shallow depth of field. No other movement.

LIGHTING: Single hard amber side-light from frame-right, heavy chiaroscuro
shadows, the rest of the room in darkness. Volumetric fog/dust visible
in the light beam.

STYLE: Black-and-white with warm amber tint on highlights only. 35mm
film grain, classical museum aesthetic, Baroque painting composition.
Cinematic texture, soft film grain.

CONSTRAINTS: sharp clarity, natural colors, stable picture, no blur,
no ghosting, no flickering. The marble texture must be realistic —
visible cracks, weathering, pitted surface. No movement on the bust
itself. No text overlays, no logos.
```

**Why it works:**
- Marble bust = zero face-drift risk (not human)
- Slow push-in is Seedance's strongest camera move
- Lighting anchor (amber side + chiaroscuro) = cinematic shield
- B&W+amber grade = stoic aesthetic code
- Audience reads "lineage of serious men" instantly

## Pattern 4: Health-Women Ritual Shot (C2 or C3, 8s)

Use case: "That Girl" wellness/health app morning routine.

```
One continuous cinematic shot, 8 seconds, 24fps, 9:16 vertical.

SCENE: A cream ceramic mug on a light wooden table, steam rising gently
from freshly poured matcha. A feminine hand with a delicate gold ring
enters frame right, picks up the mug, and lifts it toward the camera.
Linen curtains flutter softly in the background, golden sunlight filtering
through them onto the table surface.

CAMERA: Locked-off medium close-up, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field.
No camera motion.

LIGHTING: Warm golden hour sunlight from a window (frame left), diffused
through linen. Soft ambient fill from the room. No hard shadows.

STYLE: Warm cream, beige, and soft gold palette. Film-grain texture,
editorial lifestyle photography aesthetic, slightly desaturated naturals.
Cinematic texture, soft film grain.

CONSTRAINTS: sharp clarity, natural colors, stable picture, no blur,
no ghosting, no flickering. Hand movement smooth and natural. Steam
must rise realistically — subtle convection, not uniform. No text.
```

**Why it works:**
- Hand + mug = intimate parasocial framing
- Golden hour lighting = highest-leverage Seedance keyword
- Audience reads "for me" in 1 second
- Steam physics is Seedance's strength when explicit

## Pattern 5: Council/Ensemble Scene (multi-character, 8s)

Use case: ensemble frame with multiple distinct characters sitting/standing.

```
One continuous cinematic shot, 8 seconds, 24fps, 16:9 widescreen.

REFERENCES:
@Image1 = start frame (full composition anchor)
@Image2-7 = individual character reference sheets

SCENE: Wide establishing shot of [setting description — smoky backroom,
dimly lit, etc.]. Six characters seated around [object]. Each in their
native art style — [Character A: style], [Character B: style]...
The seventh chair at [position] is conspicuously empty.

ART-STYLE RULE: Each character retains their native art style from
their reference sheet throughout the shot. The mixed-media clash is
intentional. The room is rendered semi-realistic cinematic, while the
characters live in their own distinct style tradition.

CAMERA: [One continuous motion — orbital / dolly / crane]. Timed across
the 8 seconds. No cuts, no zoom.

CHARACTER MICRO-ACTIONS (exact timestamps — everything else still):
• [0.Ns — Character X: tiny action]
• [N.Ns — Character Y: tiny action]
• [single major kinetic beat at specific timestamp]

LIGHTING: Single key light (pendant/window/practical), heavy low-key
shadows, amber/teal color grade, volumetric cigarette smoke drifting
through light rays, dust particles visible in beams.

CONSTRAINTS: sharp clarity, natural colors, stable picture, no blur,
no ghosting, no flickering. Character identity stable — preserve exact
likeness from reference sheets. No dialogue, no mouth movement, no
characters leaving their chairs. No additional characters appear.
```

**Why it works:**
- Mixed-media style rule prevents unification drift
- Reference sheets lock each character
- Micro-actions with timestamps give Seedance clean motion targets
- Single camera move + still characters = high consistency

## Pattern 6: Product-Macro-with-Transformation (C1 or C5, 8s)

Use case: product reveal, showing object coming alive / transforming.

```
One continuous cinematic shot, 8 seconds, 24fps, 9:16 vertical.

SCENE: Extreme macro of [product] on a dark reflective surface. Over the
8 seconds, [transformation description — light emerging from within,
surface coming alive, etching appearing, liquid filling, dust clearing
to reveal]. The transformation begins at 1 second and completes by 7
seconds.

CAMERA: Locked-off macro, 100mm lens, very shallow depth of field.
No camera motion.

LIGHTING: Two-point — cold rim-light from frame-right separating the
product from background, warm practical ember glow from within the
product itself as transformation completes. Volumetric fog / light
atmosphere.

STYLE: Editorial product photography aesthetic, shallow DOF, muted
desaturated palette with one dominant accent color. 35mm film grain.

CONSTRAINTS: sharp clarity, natural colors, stable picture, no blur,
no ghosting, no flickering. Surface textures realistic — visible micro-
scratches, metal grain, etc. No hands, no figures, no text. The
transformation must feel physical — not digital overlay.
```

**Why it works:**
- Macro + still camera = zero AI-video tells
- Transformation is Seedance's strength (mass-and-force physics)
- Product stays hero throughout

## Pattern 7: Enemy/Tension Frame (C2, 8s)

Use case: paint the "them" / the enemy / what we're against.

```
One continuous cinematic shot, 8 seconds, 24fps, 9:16 vertical.

SCENE: [Description of the enemy archetype — corporate office at
dusk, empty cubicles under fluorescent light / the bank's logo
backlit on a skyscraper / fast food wrappers scattered in gray
parking lot at dawn]. The frame is static and oppressive. Over the
8 seconds, subtle decay — a light flickers, a logo slowly fades,
wind blows debris across the frame.

CAMERA: Static wide, 35mm lens. Very slight forward creep (0.5m
over 8s). Nothing else.

LIGHTING: Cold, flat, unflattering. Fluorescent green-white or
overcast desaturated gray. NO warm light. Sickly pallor.

STYLE: Documentary handheld-adjacent (but locked off). Muted
desaturated palette, slight color cast toward sickly-green.
35mm film grain.

CONSTRAINTS: sharp clarity, natural colors, stable picture, no blur,
no ghosting, no flickering. No figures visible. No legible brand
names or logos (use generic substitutes). Subtle motion only.
```

**Why it works:**
- Visual enemy coded clearly to tribe
- Desaturated palette creates contrast with upcoming warm/color hero clips
- No figures = no character drift risk
- Static shot = cheap but effective

## Pattern 8: Close-Up Reaction Shot (brand film C5-C6, 8s)

Use case: human reaction moment in a narrative film.

```
One continuous cinematic shot, 8 seconds, 24fps, 9:16 vertical.

REFERENCES:
@Image1 = character reference sheet

SCENE: Extreme close-up of [character — use verbatim identity line from
character ref]. Their face fills 70% of the frame. They are still,
looking slightly off-camera at [implied object/event offscreen]. Over
the 8 seconds, a subtle emotional shift — [single micro-expression
change: eye flicker, jaw tighten, brow relax, corner-of-mouth lift].
The shift happens once, around 5 seconds in.

CAMERA: Locked-off extreme close-up, 85mm lens equivalent, very shallow
DOF. Camera is completely still. No motion.

LIGHTING: Single soft key from 45 degrees left, rim light from behind
right catching edge of face and hair. Muted fill. Warm tungsten tone
with cool shadows. Chiaroscuro.

STYLE: Cinematic film-frame composition, 35mm film grain. Slightly
desaturated naturals. Editorial portrait aesthetic.

CONSTRAINTS: sharp clarity, natural colors, stable picture, no blur,
no ghosting, no flickering. Character face stable without deformation.
No mouth movement, no speaking, no mouthing words. Single subtle
expression shift only at 5s. No text, no overlays.
```

**Why it works:**
- Face-dominant frame + still camera = max character consistency
- Single micro-expression shift at timestamp = Seedance handles this cleanly
- Extreme close-up hides environment drift

## Meta-Pattern: The Negative Prompt Block

Append this to every clip prompt. It's the single most effective "clean-up" tool.

```
CONSTRAINTS (the quality suffix): sharp clarity, natural colors, stable
picture, no blur, no ghosting, no flickering, cinematic texture,
soft film grain.

DO NOT: no dialogue, no mouth movement unless specified, no characters
leaving the frame, no hard cuts, no camera shake, no jitter, no zoom-in
or zoom-out unless specified, no unified art style in multi-character
scenes, no additional characters appearing, no bright or flat lighting,
no modern UI elements, no phones, no screens, no text overlays, no
logos, no captions.
```

## Time-Coded Multi-Shot (single generation, 15s)

When the content benefits from continuous in-clip beats:

```
(0-4s) wide establishing shot, static camera, misty bamboo forest at
dawn, golden light filtering through mist, volumetric fog drifting

(4-9s) medium shot, slow dolly push-in, the fighter steps forward, silk
kimono billowing from the draft of movement, eyes fixed on something
offscreen

(9-15s) close-up, orbit shot over 6 seconds, the fighter strikes —
single blade motion, slow motion 25% speed during the strike, debris
and spark particles flying, amber rim-light catching the blade
```

Use only when all three beats genuinely connect. Never for multi-clip videos (each clip is its own generation).
